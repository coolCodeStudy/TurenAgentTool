## Interpretation contract

1. A relationship is directed from the `source_entity_id` on the left to the `target_entity_id` on the right. The direction is repeated in the `type / direction` field so an importer need not infer it.
2. `Exact assertion` is the maximum supported claim. Importers must not add unreported customer identity, supplier share, capacity, revenue attribution, delivery schedule, or operating status.
3. `disclosed_fact` records an explicit historical or present disclosure; `company_guidance` records a forward-looking company statement; `management_claim` records an issuer or program operator's technical or operating characterization; `inferred_exposure` is a curator derivation and always names premise assertion IDs.
4. Lifecycle values in this artifact are limited to `operating`, `committed`, `under_development`, `sampling`, `mass_production`, `qualification`, and `unknown`.
5. Confidence inputs are ordered as authority, explicitness, corroboration, temporal fit, geography fit, extraction method, and contradiction check. Independent review is pending, so explicit primary-source rows are capped at `medium`; the derived row is labeled `inference`.
6. Source tiers: `T1` = regulator-hosted issuer filing; `T2` = issuer investor relations, newsroom, or official announcement; `T3` = official industry-program or standards body.
7. `valid_to=unknown` is intentional and denotes that the source did not establish an end date.

## Review and update rules

1. Review at the assertion-evidence pair level. A reviewer must open the direct official URL, navigate to the exact locator, confirm assertion wording, kind, direction, lifecycle, valid time, geography, and limitation, and record reviewer identity and review date.
2. A row may move from `curated_pending_review` to `reviewed_for_implementation` only after the review in rule 1. Publication remains a separate release decision and must not be inferred from review.
3. Recheck all `company_guidance`, `committed`, `under_development`, `sampling`, and `unknown` lifecycle rows at every release cutoff. Supersede rather than silently overwrite an earlier assertion when status changes.
4. Recheck undated `SRC-016` at every release cutoff and save a content fingerprint in the implementation's evidence store. If its scope changes, create a new evidence version.
5. Preserve every assertion's original valid interval and evidence. New disclosures create new assertion/evidence versions; they do not erase historical state.
6. Do not upgrade `medium` to `high` without independent primary-source review. Do not upgrade an `inference` because its premises were reviewed; the inferred assertion remains labeled as inference.
7. Add bilateral edges only when an official source names both parties and the exact relationship. Membership, ecosystem listings, compatible products, or aggregate market commentary are insufficient.
8. Amounts, capacity, revenue, and operating status must retain qualifiers such as “approximately,” “up to,” “guided,” “expected,” “parts,” or “sampling.”
9. If a direct URL breaks, mark the evidence `source_unavailable`, retain the last verified locator and retrieval date, and do not substitute an aggregator as authoritative evidence.
10. Importers must reject relationship rows missing any required field, any inference row without premise assertion IDs, duplicate stable IDs, or any review state other than the allowed workflow states.
